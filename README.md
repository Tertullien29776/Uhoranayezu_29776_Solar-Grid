Problem StatementRural off-grid communities across East Africa increasingly depend on solar micro-grids for electricity access. However, micro-grid utility operators face two major operational bottlenecks:Financial Sustainability & Payment Defaults: Traditional post-paid or manual billing processes lead to high default rates, delayed revenues, and uncollected debts.Unmonitored Battery Failure: Storage battery banks represent over 40% of initial capital investment. Lack of real-time monitoring of Depth of Discharge (DoD), cell temperature, and State of Health (SoH) results in premature battery failures, costing thousands of dollars in emergency replacements and causing extended power outages.2. Proposed SolutionThis project implements an integrated, 3NF-normalized Oracle Database with PL/SQL business logic to manage solar micro-grid billing and storage health. The system automates prepaid credit calculations, processes hourly power consumption, instantly enforces virtual meter disconnections when credit drops to zero, and continuously tracks battery health metrics (voltage, temperature, cycle counts) to trigger automated preventive maintenance alerts before hardware failure.3. Planned Tables (3NF Design)CUSTOMERS — Customer profiles, installation addresses, contact details, and account creation dates.METERS — Smart meter metadata, assigned customer ID, micro-grid zone, and connection status (ACTIVE / DISCONNECTED).PREPAID_ACCOUNTS — Customer credit balances ($\$$), unit tariff rates ($\$/kWh$), and cumulative $kWh$ consumed.ENERGY_TRANSACTIONS — Mobile money top-up records, payment reference codes, amount paid, and timestamp.CONSUMPTION_LOGS — Hourly meter readings logging $kWh$ used, peak voltage, and reading timestamp.BATTERY_BANKS — Micro-grid battery bank specifications, rated capacity ($Ah$), nominal voltage, and installation date.BATTERY_HEALTH_LOGS — Periodic sensor readings: Voltage, Current, Temperature ($\text{°C}$), $SoC\ (\%)$, and calculated $SoH\ (\%)$.MAINTENANCE_ALERTS — Automated logs for low-balance meter cuts, over-temperature flags, and battery replacement tickets.5. Planned PL/SQL FeaturesProceduresprocess_prepaid_topup: Credits user account and automatically reactivates suspended meter connections.deduct_hourly_consumption: Deducts credit balance based on hourly $kWh$ usage and logs transaction metrics.Functionscalculate_battery_soh: Computes current battery State of Health ($SoH\ \%$) using historical charge cycles and operating temperature.check_customer_balance: Returns remaining credit and estimated days of electricity remaining.Cursorsc_depleted_accounts: Iterates over customer accounts with zero balance to trigger disconnection events.c_critical_batteries: Queries battery banks exhibiting $SoH < 70\%$ for technical dispatch.Triggerstrg_auto_disconnect: Database trigger that sets meter status to DISCONNECTED as soon as account credit reaches $\$0.00$.trg_flag_battery_warning: Inserts an automated alert into MAINTENANCE_ALERTS if battery temperature exceeds $45\text{°C}$ or $SoC$ drops below $20\%$.trg_audit_balance_changes: Audits all manual and system-driven credit adjustments for security tracking.Packagespkg_microgrid_billing: Encapsulates all tariff, top-up, rating, and disconnect logic.pkg_battery_telemetry: Encapsulates health calculation models, alert triggers, and maintenance workflows.6.
